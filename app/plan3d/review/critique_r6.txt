PAIR 1
Improved: yes. Real window with mullions, sky gradient, baseboard, and a real TV unit model replace flat white boxes. Wall and floor read as surfaces now.

Fixes:
1. Window glass: the view is a flat gray/blue gradient with a hard horizon band. Use an HDRI or skydome with a real horizon and a MeshPhysicalMaterial transmission pane (or additive sky plane) with slight reflectivity. Add the sky as scene.environment.
2. Wall and window frame are almost the same white-gray. Add reveal depth (window sill, 10-15 cm jamb returns) and subtle paint roughness variation (roughnessMap noise, bump 0.02).
3. Floor parquet: the scale looks too large and has no gloss or reflection. Reduce tile repeat to real plank size (~9 cm), add a roughnessMap, set envMapIntensity ~0.4, and use anisotropy 16.
4. Lighting is flat. Add a sun DirectionalLight through the window with a PCFSoft/VSM shadow (radius 4, bias -0.0005) and a warm RectAreaLight at the window for fill. Switch to ACESFilmic tone mapping with sRGB output.
5. Baseboard and sill are razor-sharp. Bevel the trim profile (ExtrudeGeometry bevel or a profiled shape). Add a contact-shadow blob under the TV unit, since it floats visually.

PAIR 2
Improved: yes. The room now has depth, a window with sunlight and shadow patch, baseboards, and a wood floor. BEFORE was a blown-out white door slab.

Fixes:
1. Ceiling reads dark brown, as if the wrong material or a lighting artifact. Set it to matte white with a ceiling bounce light (hemisphere light, ground color warm), or raise ambient and add a lightmap.
2. Walls are a flat gray-taupe with a muddy cast. Rebalance the exposure and light color temperature, and use a neutral-white albedo around 0.85. Add fake bounce via a hemisphere light with intensity ~0.6.
3. Window light: the sun patch has hard, aliased edges and no bloom feel. Increase shadow map to 4096, use VSM or PCSS-style blur, and add a faint additive volumetric cone plane or a soft glow sprite at the window.
4. Window glass is opaque gray-white with no outside. Add an exterior backdrop (HDRI or skydome plus a simple building silhouette) and slight Fresnel reflection.
5. The wall corner on the left is a thick column with no trim. Add a crown or ceiling line, door casing detail, and a wall/floor junction shadow gradient. Replace the dashed outline lines (debug artifacts) or hide them.
